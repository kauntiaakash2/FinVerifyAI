# Discovery and User Validation Plan
Status: Proposed research, not completed.

## Interview protocol (5–10 prospects)
Recruit analysts, finance faculty/students who fact-check filings, journalists, and compliance/research assistants (without claiming regulated suitability). Ask for last three times they checked a financial claim, time spent, input documents, wrong-result consequences, source trust requirements, current tools, and willingness to trial. Do NOT lead with your solution.

## Artifact collection
With consent obtain 30–50 anonymized real examples; record company, date, desired filing, expected result, provenance, and licensing/retention permission. Avoid proprietary documents initially.

## Alternative evaluation
Compare manually finding a 10-K/10-Q with SEC EDGAR, issuer investor relations, financial data portals, and available AI fact-checking workflows. Record actions, time, errors and costs rather than broad claims of novelty.

## Experiment plan
Randomize task order; time human completion with/without prototype; separately measure error severity, confidence in evidence and repeat-use intent. Small studies are directional, not proof of market demand.

## Go/no-go
Proceed only if the target user problem is recurrent, users trust primary filing citations, and there's measurable value beyond direct EDGAR lookup. Produce DISCOVERY_FINDINGS.md listing direct quotes (with permission), disconfirming findings and decisions.
